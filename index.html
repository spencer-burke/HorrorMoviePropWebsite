Molak is believed to originate from the death of an unnamed scholar who perished alone in a forgotten library, surrounded by texts now reduced to dust.

No records identify the civilization responsible for the manuscripts where its name appears. The language remains partially untranslated, and the estimated date ranges anywhere from 3000 BCE to the early Iron Age, depending on the source you consult.

What little has been recovered suggests Molak is not worshiped, invoked, or feared in the usual sense...

Case File #12 - "The Archivist"

Location: Private collection, Kraków

Year: 1987

Summary: A historian cataloging an estate's book collection vanished after reporting “ink on the pages moving like insects.” Room was intact. Only item missing: an unidentified manuscript fragment.

Case File #37 - University Storage Vault

Location: Oregon

Year: 2009

Summary: Graduate student working on digitizing ancient texts disappeared during their shift. Security footage glitches the moment they pick up an unmarked folio. Laptop was left open on a page describing "the hungry silence between words."

Case File #44 - The Basement Library

Location: Abandoned research facility, Bulgaria

Year: 2016
